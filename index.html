The Reset Gym

Phone habits run on a stressed, restless body. Each station here trains your nervous system to settle without a screen. Short reps, done often, build the muscle.

Reaching for your phone right now? Run the three-station craving circuit. It takes about four minutes.

Breathe

Slow exhales tell your body it is safe. Pick a pattern and follow the circle for one minute.

Ready

Ground

Anxious scrolling pulls attention into a tiny screen. Give it back to the room. Tap each time you find one.

Release

Stress stores tension in the body. Move through five 20-second holds and let go.

Ready when you are.

Ride the urge

A craving rises, peaks and falls, usually within a couple of minutes. Your job is to stay put and watch it pass without acting.

1:30
Set the phone face down, out of reach.

Feel

Under the urge to scroll there is usually a feeling. Naming it precisely helps the body settle. Tap the wheel, then follow your feeling inward.

Trigger journal

Write it down right after a scroll session, or right after you resisted one. Patterns show up on paper.

My grounding statement

Write one or two sentences in your own voice. Read it aloud when the urge shows up. It appears on the craving circuit and on your tree.

The tree

Each leaf is a reason you reach for your phone. Name it, then let it fall. What falls feeds the roots: who you are and what you want to reclaim.
